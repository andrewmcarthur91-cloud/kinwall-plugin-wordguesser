2. BRITISH NATIONAL CORPUS FREQUENCY DATA

The daily answer candidates were curated using frequency information from:

British National Corpus frequency list
University Centre for Computer Corpus Research on Language,
Lancaster University.

Source:
https://ucrel.lancs.ac.uk/bncfreq/lists/1_2_all_freq.txt

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 UK (CC BY-SA 2.0 UK).

The frequency data was used to help select and review a smaller set of
everyday five-letter answer candidates. The original frequency list is not
included in this package.
